# Duofin

Subtitle: Jellyfin, designed for Duo

Duofin brings your Jellyfin library to iPhone Duo with adaptive browsing and playback designed around the way you hold your device.

Browse on the outer display, open up for more room, or use laptop pose to keep the video above the fold and playback controls below. Original and Fill modes let you choose how your video uses the available space.

Connect to your own Jellyfin server to browse and play your films and series. Choose the MPV or native player, select audio and subtitle tracks, and continue watching across your server's supported clients. Glass controls respond to iOS accessibility settings.

Duofin is free and open source, based on Swiftfin. It is an independent app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Jellyfin. A compatible Jellyfin server and account are required. No media subscription or commercial films are included.
